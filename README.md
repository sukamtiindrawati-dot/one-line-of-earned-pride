One line, 167 characters, that gives the judge a quiet, earned moment of
pride for someone in a scene — entered in the make.fun bounty
[One Line of Earned Pride](https://make.fun/c/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557).

## The line

> She'd failed the driving test four times; this morning she parked her
> mother's old car perfectly, first try, and sat there a minute with both
> hands still on the wheel.

A long effort finally landing, in one concrete scene, with the pride felt
in the last image rather than stated anywhere in the line.

## How it works

`main.py` scores a shortlist of fresh candidate lines against the judge's
five criteria (genuine earned pride, concrete scene, pride earned inside
the line, landing in one read, freshness), picks the highest score, checks
the 1–240 character / single-line gate, and writes the winner to
`/output/entry.txt`.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
